03 / Mark

I care about the shot because I care about what the shot makes people believe.

I’m Mark Krizsan, a photographer and filmmaker working across portraiture, campaigns, and documentary storytelling. I care less about making something merely polished than making the right thing felt. Every assignment starts with the same question: what should someone feel, understand, or remember when they see this?

Before we shoot

A few things
worth knowing.

You do not need a finished creative brief before reaching out. Bring the objective. We can build the right production around it.

Do I need a fully developed concept? +

No. If you know what the work needs to accomplish, that is enough to start. Creative direction, references, locations, shot structure, and the overall visual approach can be developed as part of the project.

What do projects typically cost? +

Most commissioned projects begin around $1,000 and are scoped around the idea, production needs, location, usage, and deliverables. Smaller, tightly scoped assignments that can be completed quickly may begin around $500.

Can you handle both photography and film? +

Yes. Some assignments need one medium; others are stronger when photography and motion are designed together from the beginning. I’ll recommend the simplest production that actually serves the idea.

Do you travel? +

Yes. I take local and travel assignments. When a project requires travel, crew, equipment, permits, or location costs, those are scoped clearly before the project is booked.
