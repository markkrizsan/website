Before you book

The questions that actually matter.

Do I have to leave Squarespace, Wix or WordPress? +
No. Platform is a business decision, not an aesthetic religion. If your current system supports important workflows, we can keep it. If it is a simple marketing site and a custom rebuild is cleaner, I will recommend that instead.
What exactly does the $1,500 cover? +
A focused 1–5 page marketing site: strategy, copy refinement, custom design, responsive development, contact flow, launch setup, basic technical SEO and one revision round.
What happens after the 15-minute call? +
If the project fits the sprint, you receive the confirmed scope, invoice and short intake. Payment reserves the build window. Once the required assets and access are in, the clock starts.
Who owns the website and domain? +
You do. Your domain stays in your account. On custom-coded builds, the code can live in your repository and deployment account so there is no artificial dependency on me.
What if the site is more complex than a sprint? +
I will tell you on the fit call. I would rather decline the sprint or quote the real scope than squeeze a complex project into a format that makes the work worse.
