07 / BEFORE YOU BOOK

Only the questions
that change the decision.

Do I need a completely new website?+

Not necessarily. If the current platform and structure are sound, the right move may be a focused redesign. The fit call determines whether to improve what exists or rebuild cleanly.

Can you work with my current platform?+

Sometimes. Squarespace, Wix and WordPress can stay when the underlying functionality matters. Simple marketing sites are often cleaner as custom builds. Platform follows the business requirement.

What happens after the fit call?+

If the sprint fits, I send the confirmed scope and invoice the same day. Payment reserves the build window, then you complete a short intake and provide the necessary assets and access.

What if my project is bigger than the sprint?+

I will tell you. Complex projects should not be squeezed into a format designed for speed. If the scope is materially larger, it is quoted separately or I decline it.
