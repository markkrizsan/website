The thesis

Perception is part of the product.

People can only respond to what they can see and feel. The job is to make the first impression carry the weight of the real thing through light, framing, movement, direction, and edit.

04 / Mark

I care about the shot because I care about what the shot makes people believe.

Mark Krizsan is an independent photographer, filmmaker, and visual director working across portraiture, campaigns, and documentary storytelling. The approach starts with the same question every time: what does this image need to make someone feel, understand, or remember?
