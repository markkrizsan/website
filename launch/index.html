08 / BEFORE YOU REQUEST IT

FAQ.

The questions worth answering before money changes hands.

01

Do you guarantee more sales?
No. The Sprint is designed to strengthen clarity, credibility, perceived value and ease of action. Demand, traffic, pricing, reputation and market response remain outside my control.
02

Is this for new launches, relaunches, or both?
Both. The important condition is that the paid offer already substantially exists and there is a real launch or relaunch target. This is not a five-day exercise in inventing the business from zero.
03

How formed does my offer need to be?
Formed enough that we know what is being sold, who it is for, what the buyer pays, what truthful proof supports it and what action they need to take. I can sharpen the commercial presentation; I am not pretending an undefined offer is launch-ready.
04

Do I need finished sales copy?
No. Messaging and primary sales-page copy are part of the Sprint. You do need accurate offer information, legitimate source material and evidence for the claims being made.
05

What do you need from me before Day 1?
The formed offer, truthful proof and source material, required platform access, the target launch window, the intended distribution path and one decision-maker who can respond quickly. Preflight exists so the five production days are spent producing, not hunting for missing inputs.
06

Is the five-business-day timeline guaranteed?
It is the production target once preflight is complete and the agreed inputs, access and decisions are ready. Late assets, delayed approvals, platform problems or material scope changes can move the finish. If I see a timing risk before we start, I will call it before you reserve the Sprint.
07

What platforms can this work with?
Platform fit is confirmed before the Sprint is reserved. The standard scope can connect the core page to a straightforward checkout, booking, application or enrollment path. Complex migrations, custom applications, unusual backends or large systems work are separate scope.
08

Why not hire a cheaper developer?
If you already know exactly what the page should say, what proof matters, how the buyer journey should work, what it should look like and what needs implementing, that may be the smarter purchase. This Sprint is for launches where the commercial thinking and implementation need to move together.
09

Does this include email or a full launch campaign?
Not by default. The standard engagement owns the primary buyer-facing sales experience. Email, paid media, broader funnel architecture, course delivery systems or additional campaign assets can be scoped separately when they are genuinely needed.
10

How do payment, review and revisions work?
The standard Sprint is $3,000: $1,500 reserves the agreed start window, then the decision map and creative direction are reviewed at the direction checkpoint. The remaining $1,500 is due before production continues. The Sprint includes a consolidated review within the agreed scope rather than open-ended revision rounds, and material implementation defects attributable to my work are corrected without additional charge.
11

What happens after I request a Sprint?
I review the launch context first. If the buyer-facing experience is actually the weak link, we confirm fit, scope and a start window before anything is reserved. If I think the problem is somewhere else, I will tell you rather than forcing the Sprint into a situation it does not solve.
